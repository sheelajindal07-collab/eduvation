# Outcome instrument: decision-quality recall (TRIAL-3)

## Status

**Draft. Not approved for use with real students.**

- Reviewer: `ux-qa-reviewer` — checks that nothing here leaks a score, a
  label, or a personality-type framing toward the student, and that
  item 5's scholarship-eligibility framing stays honest about the
  scorability limit described in [Known limitations](#known-limitations-and-honesty-notes).
- Hindi wording in this document is a first-pass translation by the
  drafting agent, **not** a fluent Hindi speaker. It has not been
  reviewed and must not be read aloud to a real student, or printed on
  a real paper form, until a fluent Hindi speaker has checked it for
  register, clarity to a Class 8–12 reader, and any ambiguity the
  English does not have.
- Nothing in this document is shown to a student. It is a research
  instrument for the BCION Lite team: two short recall interviews (or
  paper forms) and a scoring rubric applied afterwards, off-site, by
  the research team — never in front of the participant.

## Purpose

This instrument measures whether a student's **decision quality** —
concretely, whether they can recall specific, checkable facts about
their own plan — improves between sign-up and four weeks of using
BCION Lite. It is a research measurement tool, not a feature of the
product and not something a student ever sees scored.

Per `CLAUDE.md`'s non-negotiables, carried into this instrument
without exception:

- No rank prediction, no "you are not suited" framing, no
  personality-type label appears anywhere in the items, the rubric, or
  the administration rules.
- The five questions are presented to the student only as "a few
  questions about your plans." The student is never told there is a
  score, never told what the scoring scale is, and never shown a
  result — not at sign-up, not at week 4, not afterwards.
- Scoring (0/1/2, see below) happens later, off-site, from the
  moderator's or the student's own written answers. It is never spoken
  aloud, written on the student's copy, or otherwise surfaced to the
  student or their parent/guardian.
- Responses are collected under an anonymous participant code only
  (see [Response sheet format](#response-sheet-format)) — never a name
  or email.

## Instrument — English

Five open-recall items. These are read aloud by a moderator, or
printed on a paper form, exactly as worded below. They are **not**
multiple choice and are **not** presented as a quiz — introduce them
to the student as "a few questions about your plans so far," with no
mention of scoring or correctness.

| # | Item wording (read aloud or printed exactly as-is) |
|---|---|
| I1 | "Tell me three pathways you're considering right now." |
| I2 | "What's the total cost of your first-choice pathway — tuition plus other costs, for the whole course, not just one year?" |
| I3 | "What's the next real deadline that applies to you for this pathway — an application date, an entrance exam registration date, or a scholarship deadline, whichever comes first?" |
| I4 | "If your first-choice pathway doesn't work out, what's your backup option?" |
| I5 | "Name one scholarship you might be eligible for." |

Moderator note (not read to the student): if the student asks "which
one is correct?" or "did I get that right?", the standard neutral
response is "There's no right or wrong answer, I just want to know
what you're thinking right now" — never a hint, a correction, or an
indication of how they scored.

## Scoring rubric (English)

Applied only by the research team, after the interview, never in the
student's presence. Each item scores 0, 1, or 2, except item 5, which
can also be flagged **NS** (not scorable — see
[Known limitations](#known-limitations-and-honesty-notes)).

**I1 — three pathways.** A "pathway" here means a specific, named
track (a course plus entry route, e.g. "B.Sc Nursing," "Diploma in
Mechanical Engineering," "B.Com + CA") — not a broad stream name
("Science," "Commerce") on its own.

- **0** — Names zero or one specific pathway, or only names a broad
  stream with no specific pathway attached.
- **1** — Names exactly two distinct, specific pathways.
- **2** — Names three or more distinct, specific pathways.

**I2 — total cost of first-choice pathway.** Score against the cost
range published and verified in the platform's own content for that
pathway.

- **0** — No figure given, "I don't know," or a figure that falls
  completely outside the published range for that pathway (wrong
  order of magnitude, not just off by a margin).
- **1** — A figure that shows some grasp of scale but is clearly a
  single year's fee mistaken for the whole-course total, or omits a
  major published cost component (e.g., tuition only, no hostel/exam
  fees) while still being in the right general range.
- **2** — A whole-course total that falls within (or immediately
  adjacent to) the platform's published range for that pathway.

**I3 — next real deadline.** Score against the next upcoming deadline
shown in verified platform content for that pathway, as of the
administration date.

- **0** — No date given, "I don't know," names an unrelated event, or
  names a deadline that has already passed.
- **1** — Names the correct deadline/event type (e.g., "entrance exam
  registration") but the date given is wrong or only vague ("sometime
  next year").
- **2** — Names the correct next deadline/event and a date accurate to
  within the month shown in verified platform content.

**I4 — backup option.** A backup must be a distinct, specific pathway
from the one named as first choice in I1 (or elsewhere in the
interview).

- **0** — No backup named, "I don't have one," or names the same
  pathway as their first choice.
- **1** — Names a real, distinct pathway, but only vaguely (e.g., "some
  other engineering course") or one that is not clearly connected to
  their stated eligibility so far.
- **2** — Names a specific, distinct, real pathway that is a plausible
  alternative given their stated profile.

**I5 — a scholarship they might be eligible for.** Score against
verified, published scholarship records for that participant's stated
profile (state, category, course level, income band, as applicable).
**Read [Known limitations](#known-limitations-and-honesty-notes)
before scoring this item — a "0" is not always the right call.**

- **0** — No scholarship named, "I don't know," or names something
  that is not a real, published, verified scholarship record.
- **1** — Names a real, verified scholarship, but the match to this
  participant's actual profile is only partial or unconfirmed (e.g.,
  right state, wrong income band, or the platform's record doesn't
  specify enough to confirm either way).
- **2** — Names a real, verified scholarship whose published
  eligibility criteria are confirmed to match this participant's
  stated profile.
- **NS (not scorable)** — the platform's verified content has **no**
  scholarship record at all covering this participant's
  state/course/category combination. See
  [Known limitations](#known-limitations-and-honesty-notes) — do not
  record this as a 0.

## Scoring rubric (हिंदी अंकन मार्गदर्शिका)

**Draft translation — needs a fluent Hindi speaker's review.** यह
मार्गदर्शिका केवल शोध टीम द्वारा, साक्षात्कार के बाद, छात्र की
अनुपस्थिति में लागू की जाती है। हर प्रश्न को 0, 1, या 2 अंक मिलते हैं,
सिवाय प्रश्न 5 के, जिसे **NS** (अंकन योग्य नहीं) भी चिह्नित किया जा
सकता है।

**I1 — तीन पथ।** "पथ" का अर्थ है एक विशिष्ट, नामित ट्रैक (कोर्स +
प्रवेश मार्ग, जैसे "B.Sc नर्सिंग," "मैकेनिकल इंजीनियरिंग डिप्लोमा") —
केवल एक व्यापक स्ट्रीम का नाम ("विज्ञान," "वाणिज्य") नहीं।

- **0** — शून्य या केवल एक विशिष्ट पथ बताया, या केवल व्यापक स्ट्रीम का
  नाम बताया।
- **1** — दो अलग, विशिष्ट पथ बताए।
- **2** — तीन या अधिक अलग, विशिष्ट पथ बताए।

**I2 — पहली पसंद के पथ की कुल लागत।** उस पथ के लिए प्रकाशित और
सत्यापित लागत सीमा के अनुसार अंकित करें।

- **0** — कोई आंकड़ा नहीं दिया, "मुझे नहीं पता," या आंकड़ा प्रकाशित
  सीमा से पूरी तरह बाहर है।
- **1** — आंकड़ा पैमाने की कुछ समझ दिखाता है लेकिन स्पष्ट रूप से केवल
  एक साल की फ़ीस है (पूरे कोर्स की नहीं), या कोई बड़ा प्रकाशित खर्च
  घटक छोड़ देता है।
- **2** — पूरे कोर्स की कुल राशि प्रकाशित सीमा के भीतर (या उसके बहुत
  नज़दीक) है।

**I3 — अगली असली अंतिम तिथि।** सत्यापित प्लेटफ़ॉर्म सामग्री में दी गई
अगली अंतिम तिथि के अनुसार अंकित करें।

- **0** — कोई तारीख नहीं दी, "मुझे नहीं पता," असंबंधित घटना बताई, या
  ऐसी तिथि बताई जो पहले ही बीत चुकी है।
- **1** — सही घटना/तिथि-प्रकार बताया (जैसे "प्रवेश परीक्षा पंजीकरण")
  लेकिन तारीख ग़लत या अस्पष्ट है।
- **2** — सही अगली अंतिम तिथि और महीने तक सटीक तारीख बताई।

**I4 — वैकल्पिक विकल्प।** वैकल्पिक विकल्प पहली पसंद से अलग, विशिष्ट
पथ होना चाहिए।

- **0** — कोई वैकल्पिक विकल्प नहीं बताया, "मेरे पास नहीं है," या पहली
  पसंद जैसा ही पथ बताया।
- **1** — असली, अलग पथ बताया, पर अस्पष्ट रूप से।
- **2** — विशिष्ट, अलग, वास्तविक पथ बताया जो उनकी पात्रता के अनुसार
  संभावित है।

**I5 — पात्र छात्रवृत्ति।** उस प्रतिभागी की प्रोफ़ाइल के लिए सत्यापित,
प्रकाशित छात्रवृत्ति रिकॉर्ड के अनुसार अंकित करें। **इस प्रश्न को
अंकित करने से पहले नीचे "ज्ञात सीमाएँ" अनुभाग पढ़ें — "0" हमेशा सही
विकल्प नहीं होता।**

- **0** — कोई छात्रवृत्ति नहीं बताई, "मुझे नहीं पता," या कोई ऐसी चीज़
  बताई जो असली, प्रकाशित, सत्यापित रिकॉर्ड नहीं है।
- **1** — असली, सत्यापित छात्रवृत्ति बताई, पर प्रोफ़ाइल से मेल केवल
  आंशिक या अपुष्ट है।
- **2** — असली, सत्यापित छात्रवृत्ति बताई जिसकी पात्रता शर्तें
  प्रतिभागी की प्रोफ़ाइल से पुष्ट रूप से मेल खाती हैं।
- **NS (अंकन योग्य नहीं)** — प्लेटफ़ॉर्म के सत्यापित कंटेंट में इस
  प्रतिभागी के राज्य/कोर्स/श्रेणी संयोजन को कवर करने वाला कोई
  छात्रवृत्ति रिकॉर्ड ही नहीं है। इसे 0 के रूप में दर्ज न करें — नीचे
  "ज्ञात सीमाएँ" देखें।

## Administration rules

**Two administration points, identical wording both times, in
whichever language (English or Hindi) the participant's consent record
specifies:**

- **T0 — at sign-up.** Administered before or immediately at sign-up,
  before the student has spent meaningful time exploring pathways in
  BCION Lite, so it captures a true baseline.
- **T1 — week 4.** The same five items, same wording, same language as
  T0 for that participant, administered again around four weeks after
  sign-up.

**Mode.** Either of the following, chosen for operational convenience
per cohort/session — never mixed for the same participant between T0
and T1:

- **Moderator reads aloud.** The moderator reads each item exactly as
  worded, waits for a free-text spoken answer, and writes down the
  answer verbatim (not paraphrased, not corrected). If the student
  clearly didn't understand the question, the moderator may repeat it
  once, unchanged, with the neutral line: "Could you say that again in
  your own words?" No further prompting, no hints, no examples.
- **Paper form.** The student writes their own free-text answers on a
  printed form using the exact item wording above. No word bank, no
  multiple-choice options, no examples printed on the form.

**Moderator conduct (both modes).**

- No calculator, phone, textbook, or the BCION Lite app open during
  administration — this measures unaided recall.
- The moderator does not hint, correct, confirm, or react visibly
  (nodding, "good," frowning) to any answer.
- "I don't know" or no answer is a valid, scorable response (see
  rubric, 0). Allow up to roughly 60 seconds of silence per item before
  recording "no answer" and moving to the next item. The whole
  interview should take under five minutes.
- The moderator never scores in the student's presence, never says a
  number or a word like "score," "correct," or "wrong" during or after
  administration, and never tells the student or a parent/guardian the
  result of either administration.
- Scoring happens later, off-site, ideally by someone other than the
  administering moderator, working from the recorded verbatim answers
  and the rubric above — not from memory of the interview.

**Framing given to the student (both time points, verbatim in
substance):** "I'm going to ask you a few questions about your plans
so far. There's no right or wrong answer — I just want to know what
you're thinking right now." Nothing about scoring, comparison to other
students, correctness, or a second visit's purpose ("we're checking if
you improved") is ever said to the student.

## Response sheet format

One row per administration (so each participant contributes one T0 row
and one T1 row). Never record a name, email, phone number, or
school-issued ID on this sheet — only the anonymous participant code
assigned at consent.

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `participant_code` | Anonymous code from the consent record. Never a name or email. |
| `administration_point` | `T0` or `T1`. |
| `date` | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `language_used` | `English` or `Hindi`, per the consent record. |
| `mode` | `moderator-read-aloud` or `self-administered-paper`. |
| `moderator_code` | Staff code of the person who administered (not who will score). |
| `i1_response` … `i5_response` | Verbatim free-text answer for each item, transcribed exactly, including "don't know" / no answer. |
| `i1_score` … `i5_score` | Filled in later, off-site: `0`, `1`, `2`, or (item 5 only) `NS`. |

| `scoring_date` | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `notes` | Free text for anything relevant to interpreting the row (e.g., "repeated I3 once," "answered partly in English, partly in Hindi," "loud environment, may have missed I2"). Never a score, a label, or a personality-type note. |

## Known limitations and honesty notes

- **Item 5 scorability depends on content coverage, not just student
  knowledge.** A "0" on item 5 should mean the student could not name
  an eligible scholarship. It should **not** silently absorb the case
  where the platform's verified content library simply has no
  scholarship record covering this participant's
  state/course/category combination — in that case, no answer could
  ever have scored a 2, so a low score would measure the content
  register's coverage, not the student's decision quality. Use the
  **NS** flag for that case (see rubric) and track the NS rate as a
  separate content-coverage metric, not as part of the item-5 score
  distribution. A high NS rate is a signal to add scholarship records,
  not a signal that the instrument or the students are failing.
- **Hindi wording is a draft, not a validated translation.** It has
  not been reviewed by a fluent Hindi speaker for register, natural
  phrasing for a Class 8–12 reader, or back-translation accuracy. Do
  not administer the Hindi version to a real student until that review
  has happened.
- **This is a five-item instrument for a pilot of 10–100 users**,
  intended to give the team a directional read on decision-quality
  change, not a validated psychometric scale. It is not designed to
  produce, and must never be used to produce, any per-student label,
  rank, or category shown back to the student, a parent, a school, or
  anyone outside the research team.
