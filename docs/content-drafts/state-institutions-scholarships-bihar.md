Patna Medical College and Hospital (PMCH), Patna — Bihar's oldest
government medical college (founded 1925, per secondary sources; not
confirmed from a primary source this session) — was also researched as a
candidate for this section. Its admission route is confirmed in outline
(NEET-UG, with counselling split between the Medical Counselling Committee
for the All-India Quota and BCECEB for the state quota), but reported seat
counts (185–200 depending on source) and MBBS fee totals (figures seen
ranged from roughly ₹9,000/year tuition up to a total-course figure near
₹1.48 lakh, inconsistent across secondary aggregators) could not be
reconciled or confirmed against a primary fee notification this session —
the institution's own site (`patnamedicalcollege.edu.in`) loaded but its
admissions/fee content did not render as extractable text with the tools
available this session. Given that MIT Muzaffarpur's admission *process*
was confirmable directly from the official BCECEB site while PMCH's fee
figures were not reconcilable, MIT Muzaffarpur is used as the primary
entry above; a verifier may prefer PMCH instead and should treat both as
