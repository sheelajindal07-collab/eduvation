## 2. Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes (SC), Manipur —
   Department/Directorate of Other Backward Classes & Scheduled Castes,
   Government of Manipur (centrally sponsored, state-administered)

Chosen as the state-administered post-matric scholarship scheme for
Manipur. Like the equivalent schemes documented for Assam and Haryana in
this same batch, this is a centrally sponsored scheme (funding split
90% central / 10% state, per the official page) administered through a
dedicated Manipur state department, rather than a scheme funded entirely
out of the state's own budget — that distinction is stated plainly rather

## Honest scope note

Manipur does have its own distinctly named state department administering
post-matric scholarships (Other Backward Classes & Scheduled Castes
Department) with a clearly stated income cap read directly from an
official page, so this is not a case of "no distinct infrastructure."
However, two honest gaps stand out and should not be minimized: (1) the
best-documented "government medical institution" found for Manipur (RIMS)
turned out, on the facts read this session, to be a **central** government
institute rather than a Manipur **state** government one, which is why the
state-founded engineering institution (MIT, Imphal) was chosen as the
primary answer instead; and (2) a possible purely state-funded "State
Merit Scholarship Manipur" scheme was found by name only, with its actual
terms unreachable this session — it is reported as an unconfirmed lead,
not as a verified alternative.
