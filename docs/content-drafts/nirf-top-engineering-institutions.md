## 3. How admission generally routes through JEE Main / JEE Advanced

This section describes the *general* national admission route for the
government institutes above (IITs, NITs, IIITs, GFTIs). It is not a
per-institute eligibility or cutoff record — that would need separate,
deeper research per the task brief.

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-stage exam structure | JEE Main (conducted by the National Testing Agency, NTA) is the first stage; JEE Advanced (conducted by one IIT on a rotating basis — IIT Roorkee for the 2026 cycle) is the second stage, open only to a capped number of top JEE Main rankers | https://jeeadv.ac.in/eligibility.html (JEE Advanced official eligibility page) | 2026-09-21 | High for the two-stage structure and organizing-institute detail, read directly from the official page |
| Who uses which exam for seat allocation | IIT seats are allotted on JEE Advanced rank; NIT/IIIT/GFTI seats are allotted on JEE Main rank. Both flow through one common counselling body | Secondary corroboration only this session (the official JoSAA homepage text fetched did not itself spell out the Advanced-vs-Main split in the excerpt retrieved) — https://josaa.nic.in/ for the counselling body itself; the Advanced-vs-Main split specifically needs re-confirmation from JoSAA's Business Rules/Information Bulletin PDF | 2026-09-21 | Medium — the counselling-body fact is High (read from josaa.nic.in directly); the exact "IITs=Advanced, others=Main" allocation rule is Medium, stated consistently by multiple secondary sources but not read verbatim from JoSAA's own rules document this session |
