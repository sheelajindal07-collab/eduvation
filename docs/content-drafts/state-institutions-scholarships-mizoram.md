## Honest scope note

Mizoram does have its own distinct admissions/scholarship infrastructure:
a wholly state-owned medical college (ZMC&H) with its own admission
process page and fee structure, and a dedicated state body (the Mizoram
Scholarship Board) that issues its own scholarship notices and paper
application forms distinct from the central NSP flow. This is not a case
of a small state with no infrastructure of its own. The main gap in this
draft is precision, not absence: the official PMMS application form
itself does not publish an income cap or a fixed amount, and it directly
contradicts secondary "explainer" sites on who is currently eligible
(ST-only per the 2026-27 form vs. "any permanent resident" per several
aggregator pages) — both points need a human verifier to resolve against
the Mizoram Scholarship Board directly, ideally by phone/in person given
no clear online portal was found for this scheme.
