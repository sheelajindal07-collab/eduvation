## Scope note

This covers the general, publicly documented route for an Indian Class 12
student pursuing **undergraduate** study in Japan: (1) student visa /
status-of-residence basics, (2) the general application route and typical
timeline, (3) the MEXT (Japanese Government) Scholarship as it applies to

magnitude. It does not cover any specific university's admission
requirements, specific department cut-offs, or India-specific procedural
detail beyond what could be confirmed this session (see the "Not

## 3. MEXT (Japanese Government / Monbukagakusho) Scholarship — "Undergraduate student" category

The MEXT scholarship has seven category types; the one relevant to a
Class 12 student entering a Japanese bachelor's degree fresh from school
is the **"Undergraduate student" (学部留学生)** category. Figures below are
for that category specifically, taken directly from the scholarship
comparison table on the official portal — care was taken to match the
right column, since the same table lists a much higher figure (¥242,000/
month) that belongs to a **different** category (Young Leaders Program,
a graduate-level scheme), not to undergraduates.

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two application routes | (1) **Embassy recommendation** — applicant is recruited/screened by the Japanese Embassy/Consulate in their home country; (2) **University recommendation** — a MEXT-approved Japanese university recruits and recommends the candidate directly (requires an existing arrangement between that Japanese university and, often, a partner institution — not generally available to an independent applicant with no such tie) | https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/scholarships/mext-scholarships/ | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the raw page's HTML table, not a summarised version |
| Age / academic eligibility (Undergraduate student category) | Under 25 years of age, and have completed 12 years of school education, or completed education at a school comparable to a Japanese high school (prospective graduates included) | Same page | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Monthly stipend (Undergraduate student category) | **¥117,000 per month** (the ¥143,000–145,000 figure on the same table is for Research/Teacher-training students, and ¥242,000 is for the separate, graduate-level Young Leaders Program — neither applies to the Undergraduate category) | Same page | 2026-09-21 | High — confirmed by reading the table's column groupings directly in the raw HTML, cross-checked against the "Field of study" and "Level of studies" rows to confirm which figure aligns to "Undergraduate students" |
