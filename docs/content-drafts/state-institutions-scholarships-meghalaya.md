Technology (RIST)" at Byrnihat and three state polytechnics — Shillong,

Note on alternatives considered: the same Directorate of Higher & Technical
Education "Scholarship Schemes" index
(https://megeducation.gov.in/dhte/pages/scholarship_schemes.html) also
lists a separate, differently-named "Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST
Students (CSS)" and a "State Merit Scholarship," among others. The "CSS"
label on the SC/ST post-matric scheme suggests it is a Centrally Sponsored
Scheme administered by the state (like the NSP-hosted schemes seen in other
states' drafts) rather than a purely state-run scheme, which is why the
Post-Matric Merit Scholarship for Tribal Students above — which explicitly
requires Meghalaya residency and reads as state-specific — was picked as
this file's primary answer. The state also separately runs a "Chief
Minister's Scholarship Scheme, 2025" via https://meghalayaone.gov.in/ ,
whose own eligibility/income/amount detail was not opened this session and
would be a reasonable second scheme for a future research pass. The state
also references Pre/Post-Matric ST and SC scholarship notice PDFs
(`Adv PMS ST 2526.pdf`, `Adv PMS SC 2526.pdf`) linked from the education
department homepage, neither of which was opened this session.

No "neither programme exists" finding applies here: Meghalaya has both an
identifiable government engineering institution (NIT Meghalaya, a central
Institute of National Importance, with its admission-counseling route
confirmed directly from its own site) and an identifiable state-run
post-matric scholarship for Class 11–12/UG-level Scheduled Tribe students
(the Post-Matric Merit Scholarship for Tribal Students, read directly from
the state education department's own page). What is *not* confirmed is
fine detail — NIT Meghalaya's current fee/seat figures, and whether the
scholarship's decades-looking per-month amounts are still current — all
flagged "not found" or "Medium/Low" above rather than estimated.
