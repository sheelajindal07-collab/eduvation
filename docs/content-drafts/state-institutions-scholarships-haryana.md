## Honest scope note

Haryana is not a small state with "little or no distinct admissions
infrastructure" — the opposite was found: it runs its own centralised
technical-admission body (HSTES), its own centralised scholarship portal
(Har Chhatravratti), and a separate Social Justice department scholarship
channel, all under haryana.gov.in / *.highereduhry.ac.in domains. The main
gaps in this draft are depth, not absence: exact fee/seat figures for the
engineering institution, a confirmed government medical institution with
primary-source detail, and a reconciliation of the two conflicting
income-cap figures for the post-matric scholarship (₹2.5 lakh vs ₹1 lakh)
found on different official Haryana pages.
