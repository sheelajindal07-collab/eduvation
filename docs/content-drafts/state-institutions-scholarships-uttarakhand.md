## Honest scope note

Uttarakhand does have its own identifiable state government engineering
institution (College of Technology, Pantnagar, under GBPUAT) with a
working official site describing its admission route and seat quotas,
and its own Social Welfare Department scholarship pages listing several
named post-matric schemes with income caps, amounts and deadlines. This
is not a case of a state with no distinct infrastructure. The main gaps
in this draft are: (1) a government **medical** college could not be
located at a working official domain within this session's tool budget
(several domain guesses failed to resolve), so an engineering institution
was substituted, which the task explicitly permits via "OR"; (2) the
engineering college's own fee-details page exists but its actual rupee
figures were not extractable this session, so fees are "not found" rather
than fabricated; (3) whether the Scheduled Caste Post-Matriculation
Scholarship is centrally funded (and merely state-administered) or
wholly state-funded was not confirmed from an Uttarakhand-specific
document — flagged, not guessed.
