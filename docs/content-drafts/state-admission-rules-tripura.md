# DRAFT RESEARCH — Tripura: State Admission-Rules Profile (Class 12 board,
engineering counselling, medical counselling, domicile/state-quota basics)

This is unverified draft research collected by an AI agent from public web

Research date: 2026-09-21. Researcher: automated agent (session run for
carohitjin@gmail.com), web search only, no student data used or seen.

**Scope note:** per `docs/DECISIONS.md` (2026-09-21, pilot scope widened
to all-India admission rules), this file covers Tripura's *state-level
admissions infrastructure* — the board, the counselling bodies and the
domicile rules — one level up from individual institutions, mirroring the
depth of the existing `docs/content-drafts/gujarat-institutions.md` /
`gujcet-eligibility.md` pair for Gujarat. It does **not** attempt an
institution-by-institution list. It also does not re-research NEET-UG or
JEE Main themselves (national exams; eligibility already drafted in
`docs/content-drafts/neet-ug-eligibility.md` and
`docs/content-drafts/jee-main-eligibility.md`) — only Tripura's *state
counselling* layer on top of them.

**Headline honest finding:** unlike some neighbouring north-eastern
states (see the existing Manipur draft, where no state engineering CET
exists at all), Tripura **does** run its own named state-level entrance
examination — **TBJEE (Tripura Board of Joint Entrance Examination)** —
covering engineering, agriculture, veterinary, fisheries and paramedical
courses, with a domicile rule (a "Permanent Resident of Tripura
Certificate") read directly from TBJEE's own 2026 eligibility notice, one
of the more explicit, directly-confirmed domicile rules found in this
round of state research. Medical admission runs on a **separate** state
body — TRMCC (Tripura Medical Counselling Committee), under the
Directorate of Medical Education — for NEET-UG based counselling. Tripura
does **not** appear to have a distinct "Directorate of Technical
Education" department of its own; diploma/degree engineering admission
administration was found sitting under the Directorate of Higher
Education instead, a genuine structural finding rather than a gap in this
session's search (see section 2).

## 2. Engineering-admission counselling body and process

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Does Tripura have its own state engineering CET? | **Yes** — the **Tripura Board of Joint Entrance Examination (TBJEE)**, which the board's own site states "conducts Joint Entrance Examination in the month of April for preparation of merit list" for "Engineering, Technological, Agricultural, Veterinary, Fisheries and Paramedical Courses." This is a genuine state-run common entrance test, distinct from JEE Main. | https://tbjee.tripura.gov.in/ (official site, fetched directly) | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the official TBJEE site |
| TBJEE eligibility / domicile rule (read directly from the primary PDF) | Candidate must (a) be a citizen of India; (b) be a **permanent resident of Tripura**, with the candidate or a parent having resided in Tripura continuously for **not less than 10 years** immediately before applying, evidenced by a **Permanent Resident of Tripura Certificate (PRTC)** submitted before counselling when asked; (c) have passed/appeared the H.S.(+2) exam from TBSE, CBSE, or an equivalent board in the relevant subjects. Named exceptions exist for: children of All-India-Services officers of the Manipur-Tripura cadre and children of state-government employees posted outside Tripura (eligible regardless of prior stay); children of Central-Government/deputation officers serving in Tripura ≥3 years, if the candidate passed H.S. from a Tripura institution; and wards of ex-servicemen / children of serving defence personnel hailing from Tripura, subject to a 5-year residency and passing H.S. from a recognised institution. A candidate who has already been allotted a seat via a past TBJEE and not formally surrendered it is barred from reappearing. | https://tbjee.tripura.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-02/eligibility.pdf — "Eligibility for Appearing at Tripura Joint Entrance Examination 2026," official notice, PDF opened and read directly this session | 2026-09-21 | High — read verbatim from the official 2026-cycle eligibility PDF |
