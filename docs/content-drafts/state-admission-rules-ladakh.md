Scope note: Ladakh is a Union Territory **without a legislature**
(bifurcated from the former state of Jammu and Kashmir on 31 October 2019),
not a state. This file covers UT-level admissions *infrastructure* (which
board, which counselling body, which process, what domicile means), one
level up from individual-institution profiles — mirroring the depth of
`docs/content-drafts/gujarat-institutions.md` / `gujcet-eligibility.md`,
not replacing a future institution-level file. Note upfront: this is a
genuine "small-UT" case in the sense the task description anticipated —
Ladakh has **no engineering or medical degree college of its own** (see
sections 2 and 3), and instead relies on central-government reservation
schemes to place its domicile students in institutions elsewhere in India.
It does, however, have its own school-board arrangement and its own
domicile/resident-certificate regime, which are *not* borrowed wholesale
from a neighbouring state — this needed clarifying carefully rather than
assumed either way.

## 2. Engineering-admission counselling

Ladakh has **no state-run engineering/technical degree college and no state
CET of its own**. This was independently corroborated two ways this
session: (a) the University of Ladakh's own site lists only Agricultural
Sciences & Technology, Horticultural Sciences & Technology, a Defence &
Strategic Studies programme, and a NIELIT-partnered BCA — no B.Tech/B.E.
programme; and (b) the official CSAB-NEUT scheme (below) explicitly exists
"for States/UTs lacking such facilities in the AICTE-approved
institutions." Ladakh students access engineering seats through JEE Main
plus (at least) two distinct central mechanisms:
