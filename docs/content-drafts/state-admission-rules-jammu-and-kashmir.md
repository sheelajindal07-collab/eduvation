the official brochures) fee figures. Written per the pilot-scope widening
to all-India admission rules, `docs/DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-21.

Honest scoping finding: Jammu and Kashmir is **not** a small UT with
little distinct admissions infrastructure of its own. It runs a large,
long-established stack of its own: its own Class 12 board (JKBOSE), its
own single admission-and-counselling authority for essentially all
professional courses — engineering, medical, dental, AYUSH, B.Ed,
paramedical, nursing and more — the J&K Board of Professional Entrance
Examinations (JKBOPEE), and its own detailed, UT-specific reservation
regime under the Jammu and Kashmir Reservation Act, 2004. A notable
structural feature specific to J&K (not found in most other states
researched in this series) is that JKBOPEE's mandate and several of its
reservation categories explicitly span **both** the UT of Jammu and
Kashmir **and** the UT of Ladakh, which was carved out of the former
state of Jammu and Kashmir in 2019 but, on the evidence gathered this
session, still relies on JKBOPEE for some professional-course counselling
rather than running a fully separate system of its own.

## 4. Domicile / state-quota basics for admission to J&K government institutions

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core domicile rule | Across both engineering (CET) and medical (NEET-UG state counselling), the operative document is a "Domicile Certificate of UT of J&K," with a specific alternate route for Ladakh: candidates belonging to the UT of Ladakh use their **ST (Scheduled Tribe) certificate**, issued by Ladakh's competent authority, "treated as" a domicile certificate for this purpose. This means J&K's own professional-course counselling body, JKBOPEE, currently still processes admissions for at least some Ladakh candidates (via the ST-certificate route) even though Ladakh has been a separate Union Territory since 2019 | Both official 2026 JKBOPEE brochures (CET Engineering Section 4; NEET-UG Section 9), quoted directly | 2026-09-21 | High for the rule as stated by JKBOPEE itself. Not independently verified against a Ladakh-side source (e.g. Ladakh's own Health & Medical Education Department) this session — a verifier should check whether Ladakh residents without an ST certificate have any other route, and how this interacts with Ladakh's own separate "Central Pool Ladakh Merit Seat" bond scheme found in Annexure X of the NEET-UG brochure (a Ladakh-administration document requiring 2-5 years of compulsory government service after using such a seat, which appears to be a Ladakh-specific arrangement layered on top of, not replacing, the JKBOPEE process). |
| Reservation categories and percentages (Government institutions, both engineering and medical) | Per S.O. 176 dated 15-03-2024 (read with S.O. 305 dated 21-05-2024 for medical, and S.O. 277 dated 13-08-2021 as the earlier baseline): Scheduled Caste (SC) 8%; Scheduled Tribes (ST) 20% total, split into ST-i 10% (a named list including Bakarwal, Gujjar, Gaddi, etc.) and ST-ii 10% (a separate list added in 2024, including Paddari Tribe, Pahari Ethnic Group, Gadda Brahmin, Koli); Other Backward Classes (OBC) 8%; Residents of Backward Area (RBA) 10%; Residents of areas adjoining the Actual Line of Control/International Border (ALC/IB) 4%; Economically Weaker Section (EWS) 10%; plus horizontal (cut-across) reservations of Persons with Disabilities 4%, Children of Defence Personnel (CDP) 3%, Children of Para-military Forces/UT Police Personnel (JKPM) 1%, and Sports proficiency 2%. Total vertical reservation is capped at 50% by Section 9 of the J&K Reservation Act, 2004 | Both official 2026 JKBOPEE brochures (CET Engineering Section 5; NEET-UG Section 11), and the underlying Social Welfare Department notifications reproduced as Annexures XI and XII of the NEET-UG brochure, quoted/tabulated directly | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from both official brochures and the reproduced government notifications. |
| Reservation applies to Government institutions only | Explicitly stated in both brochures: this entire reservation policy (and the associated Tuition Fee Waiver benefit for engineering) applies only in Government-run colleges "unless otherwise prescribed specifically or notified for any private College." A named exception: Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW, family income under ₹4.5 lakh/year) is separately stated to apply to **all** engineering colleges, Private as well as Government | Both official 2026 JKBOPEE brochures, CET Rule 5(a) and NEET-UG Rule (a) | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quotes. |
| Female-candidate sub-reservation (medical only) | Under Section 9 of the J&K Reservation Act, 2004, 50% of seats in **each** category (including Open Merit) for MBBS and BDS admission are further reserved for female candidates; this proviso was not found stated for engineering CET in the same brochure | Official NEET-UG brochure, Section 6(ii), quoting Section 9 of the J&K Reservation Act, 2004 verbatim | 2026-09-21 | High for medical (direct quote of the Act). Not confirmed either way for engineering — absence of the same clause in the CET brochure is not proof it doesn't apply there under some other order; a verifier should check specifically. |
| Category-certificate issuing authorities | SC/OBC/RBA/ALC-IB certificates: Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tehsildar. ST certificates: authority varies by sub-category (not fully itemised this session). CDP (Children of Defence Personnel): Commanding Officer of the unit, or Zila Sainik Welfare Officer for ex-servicemen. JKPM (children of paramilitary/UT police): DIG (Deputy Inspector General of Police) concerned. Sports-proficiency points: Secretary, J&K Sports Council. TFW/Poor-and-Backward income certificates: Tehsildar of the concerned Tehsil | Both official 2026 JKBOPEE brochures, Annexure I in each | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the official annexures. |
| Small-UT/no-distinct-infrastructure caveat check | Jammu and Kashmir is **not** a case of little/no distinct admissions infrastructure — it has one of the most developed and extensively documented state-level systems found in this research series: its own Class 12 board (JKBOSE), its own single professional-admissions authority (JKBOPEE) covering essentially every professional course, and its own detailed, actively-amended (2021, 2024) reservation regime. The one genuinely unusual structural feature, flagged repeatedly above, is that this J&K-run system still explicitly extends — via the ST-certificate-as-domicile route — to at least some candidates from the separately-administered UT of Ladakh | Sections 1-3 above | 2026-09-21 | High — directly evidenced by both official brochures and the board's own site, not inferred. |
