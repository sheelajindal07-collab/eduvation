## 2. Post Matric Scholarship to Scheduled Caste Students (Punjab)

Chosen as the "one real state-run post-matric scholarship scheme" for
Class 11–12 / UG students. It is a centrally sponsored scheme
administered in Punjab by the Department of Welfare of Scheduled Castes
and Backward Classes, matching the same "centrally sponsored, state
administered" pattern already used for Gujarat's equivalent scheme in
`docs/content-drafts/gujarat-scholarships.md`. The primary-source PDF
below carries no revision/last-updated date of its own, so how current
its figures are (especially the funding figures, which cite the 11th
five-year plan) could not be established this session.

  produce a live application system.
