## Honest scope note

Puducherry does have its own, longstanding, state-run Post-Matric
Scholarship Scheme for Class XI–XII (run by its own Directorate of School
Education since 1968-69) — this is not a case of a small UT having no
distinct infrastructure of its own; the school-level scheme is genuinely
Puducherry-specific and was read directly from the Directorate's own page.
By contrast, the exact UG-level scholarship picture (the
Higher-and-Technical-Education-administered schemes, PONSHE) was only
located, not opened, this session and needs a follow-up pass. Similarly,
IGMC&RI's status as a genuine Government-of-Puducherry medical institution
is confirmed directly from primary sources, but its seat numbers, fees,
and precise admission route (CENTAC vs. NEET-UG central counselling split)
could not be confirmed from a primary document this session and are
marked "not found" or "needs manual verification" above rather than
estimated.
