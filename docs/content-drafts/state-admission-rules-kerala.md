## 4. Domicile / state-quota basics for Kerala government institutions

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nativity/citizenship baseline | Only Indian citizens are eligible for admission to these professional courses unless otherwise notified. Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) and PIO cardholders are treated at par with Indian citizens for the limited purpose of admission only — they are **not** eligible for any reservation or fee concession (per a cited 2023 Supreme Court WP(C) judgment and a corresponding Kerala Government Order) | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 3.I | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote, including the cited case/G.O. references as given in the source. |
| Three-tier domicile categorisation | All Indian-citizen applicants are categorised as **Keralite**, **Non-Keralite Category I (NK I)**, or **Non-Keralite Category II (NK II)** — this is the operative "domicile" framework for every professional course covered by this notification (Engineering, Architecture, Pharmacy, Medical & Medical Allied) | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 3.I | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase. |
| Keralite (full domicile) | A candidate "of Kerala origin," proven via the candidate's own or a parent's SSLC place-of-birth entry, passport place-of-birth entry, a competent-authority birth certificate, a Village Officer/Tahsildar certificate, or (for children of All India Service officers allotted to Kerala cadre, who are "deemed" Keralite) a specific AIS-cadre certificate. Only Keralite candidates are eligible for Communal/Special/Persons-with-Disabilities reservation or any fee concession — AIS-officers'-children who are "deemed Keralite" are a specific exception: deemed Keralite for admission purposes but explicitly **not** eligible for reservation/fee concession. | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 3.I.A | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase of a detailed primary-source clause. |
| Non-Keralite Category I (NK I) | Not of Kerala origin, but qualifies via any one of: (1) child of a non-Keralite Central-Government/Defence-Service parent posted to Kerala, having done the qualifying course in Kerala; (2) child of a non-Keralite parent who served the Government of Kerala for at least 2 years, having done the qualifying course in Kerala; (3) resident of Kerala for 5 of the last 12 years of study; or (4) completed school studies in Kerala from Std. VIII to XII. NK-I candidates are considered against **'State Merit' seats** for Engineering/Architecture/B.Pharm/Medical & Medical Allied courses, but are **not** eligible for Communal/Special/PD reservation or fee concession. | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 3.I.B | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase. |
| Non-Keralite Category II (NK II) | Candidates meeting neither the Keralite nor NK-I criteria. Eligible for all Engineering seats CEE allots, but in Government Cost-Sharing Engineering colleges only after Keralite/NK-I candidates are unavailable (from the second phase of allotment onward); eligible for both Government and Management-quota seats in Private Self-Financing Engineering/Architecture/Siddha/Unani colleges (for Siddha/Unani specifically, only after Keralite/NK-I are unavailable); eligible for up to 10% Management-quota seats in Private Self-Financing Pharmacy colleges; **not** eligible for Medical & Medical Allied/Engineering/Pharmacy/Architecture admission in Government colleges; but eligible for the 15% domicile-blind, NEET-UG-rank-based quota in Self-Financing medical colleges described in section 3. NK-II candidates are not eligible for any reservation or fee concession. | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 3.I.C | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase. |
| NRI quota (private self-financing Medical/Dental colleges) | A candidate dependent on a defined set of relatives (parent, sibling, spouse, first cousin, etc.) working abroad may apply under the NRI quota, subject to extensive sponsor-documentation requirements (passport/visa or equivalent residency document, employment certificate, relationship certificate, notarised sworn affidavit). Per a cited 2020 Supreme Court judgment (WP(C) No. 380/2019), if an NRI-quota seat is about to lapse for want of a candidate holding a domicile certificate, admission may be made without considering the domicile certificate. | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 7 (documents section) | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase, including the cited case reference as given in the source. |
| Minority quota | Christian/Muslim minority-status Self-Financing colleges have minority-quota seats (Engineering/Architecture/Pharmacy/MBBS/BDS) requiring a Community/Minority certificate from the Village Officer (or the correctly recorded religion entry in the SSLC/educational certificate) | Official KEAM-2026 Notification, Clause 7.i | 2026-09-21 | High — direct quote/paraphrase. |
