## 5. General exam pattern

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall structure | Two stages: (1) a written examination, (2) an Intelligence and Personality Test / interview at a Services Selection Board (SSB) for candidates who qualify the written stage | Notification PDF, Appendix I, "A. Scheme of Examination" | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Written papers — IMA / INA / AFA candidates | Three objective-type (MCQ) papers: English (Code 11), General Knowledge (Code 12), Elementary Mathematics (Code 13) — each 100 marks, 2 hours duration. Written total = 300 marks | Notification PDF, Appendix I, para 2(a) | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Written papers — OTA candidates | Two objective-type (MCQ) papers: English (Code 11) and General Knowledge (Code 12) — each 100 marks, 2 hours duration. Written total = 200 marks | Notification PDF, Appendix I, para 2(b) | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Written + interview weighting | Interview/SSB marks are set equal to the written-exam marks for each course, giving grand totals of 600 for IMA, 600 for INA, 600 for AFA, and 400 for OTA | Notification PDF, Appendix I, para 2 (note after the tables) | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Question language | General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics papers are set bilingually (Hindi and English); the notification's wording implies English paper is assessed in English | Notification PDF, Appendix I, para 3 | 2026-09-21 | Medium — the bilingual statement is verbatim; the inference about the English paper's language is this agent's reading, not an explicit separate statement, so a verifier should double check |
| Standard of papers | Elementary Mathematics is set at Matriculation level; the other subjects are set at approximately the standard expected of a graduate of an Indian university | Notification PDF, Appendix I, "B. Standard and Syllabus" | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Negative marking | For each wrong answer in the objective papers, one-third (0.33) of that question's allotted marks is deducted. Giving more than one answer counts as wrong. An unattempted (blank) question has no penalty. Calculators are not permitted | Notification PDF, Appendix I, "3. Penalty for wrong Answers" and para 7 | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Handwriting requirement | Candidates must write papers in their own hand; use of a scribe is not permitted (no exception carved out in the section read) | Notification PDF, Appendix I, para 5 | 2026-09-21 | High |
