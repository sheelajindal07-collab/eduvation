Research date: 2026-09-21. Researcher: automated agent (session run for

**Naming note (important for the verifier):** the assigned topic title used
the phrase "Central Sector Scholarship / Post-Matric Scholarship for ST
students." These are not the same scheme. The scheme that actually targets
ST students by category — the one researched below — is officially named
the **"Post Matric Scholarship for the students belonging to Scheduled
Tribes for studies in India"** and, per its own 2022 regulation document, is
explicitly a **"केंद्रीय प्रायोजित योजना" = "Centrally Sponsored Scheme"
(cost-shared between Centre and States)**, administered by the Ministry of
Tribal Affairs (MoTA) — **not** a "Central Sector" scheme (a different GoI
budget category that is 100%-Centre-funded, e.g. the merit-based CSSS run
by the Department of Higher Education, which is a separate scheme open to
all categories and is documented separately in
`docs/content-drafts/gujarat-scholarships.md` section 1). A verifier should
confirm which of the two schemes the product actually intends to reference
before any fact here is promoted.

## 4. Scholarship amount

The scholarship has two components: (a) reimbursement of the compulsory,
non-refundable course fee, and (b) a monthly maintenance allowance
("stipend"), both paid for the full course duration, differentiated by a
4-tier course grouping and by hosteller vs. day-scholar status.

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
