## Scope note

This covers only the general route for an Indian Class 12 student applying
to a French public-institution undergraduate ("Licence"/Bachelor's) program:
(1) the "Etudes en France" (Campus France) enrolment procedure that India is
subject to, (2) the student long-stay visa (VLS-TS) basics, (3) the general
application route/order of steps, and (4) an order-of-magnitude cost range
(tuition + living). It does **not** cover scholarships in depth (e.g.
Eiffel, Charpak), private/business-school-specific admission processes,
post-graduation work/residency pathways, or Parcoursup mechanics in detail
— those would need separate research. A general note on a data anomaly
encountered this session is recorded at the end.

| 5-step India application route (as published) | (1) Create an account on the "Etudes en France" platform, fill personal/academic details, upload documents (CV, photo, passport, mark sheets/certificates), submit programme choices — wait for a Campus France advisor to schedule an academic interview. (2) Attend the academic interview (online or in person) with a Campus France India manager, bringing original + photocopied mandatory documents and the EEF fee payment receipt. (3) Once the file is validated, receive and respond to university admission decisions; on acceptance, print the acceptance letter and begin the visa procedure.
