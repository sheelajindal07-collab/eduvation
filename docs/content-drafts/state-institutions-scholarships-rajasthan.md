the opposite was found: it runs its own Social Justice and Empowerment
Department scholarship system (SJMS/Scholarship Portal) governed by
digitally-signed departmental orders that this session was able to open
and read directly, and its own state engineering university (MBM,
Jodhpur, a former government engineering college upgraded to a state
university in 2021). The gaps in this draft are depth and one missing

  when this task started, so nothing here comes from a keyword search —

- MBM University's Admissions, Fee Structure, AICTE-EOA and NIRF
  sub-pages are JavaScript-rendered and returned only a loading screen to
  this session's fetch tool, so the admission mechanism, fee and
  ranking/accreditation status are "not found" for the institution.
- No Rajasthan government **medical** college's official site could be
