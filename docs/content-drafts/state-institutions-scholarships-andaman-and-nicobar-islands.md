## Honest scope notes for the reviewer

1. A genuinely UT-run post-matric scholarship (the Tribal Welfare "Additional
   Scholarship" scheme above) was identified from an official source, satisfying
   the task's requirement — but only its name, administering directorate and
   target group could be confirmed this session, not its income cap, amount,
   or application portal/deadline. This is a materially thinner scholarship
   entry than the institution entry above, or than the Goa draft's scholarship
   entry (which was read from the scheme's own dedicated official notice, not
   a secondhand description inside a college prospectus). A reviewer must
   locate and open the Directorate of Tribal Welfare's own page (likely on the
   `and.nic.in` domain family, which was entirely unreachable from this
   session — see Method note above) before any income/amount figure can be
   published.
2. This session could not confirm whether Andaman & Nicobar Islands has *any*
   distinct UT-run, non-tribal-specific post-matric scholarship (i.e. one open
   to all A&N students generally, the way Gujarat's SEBC/ST schemes or Goa's
   Gagan Bharari scheme are caste/tribe-specific-but-still-state-run). Only a
   tribe-specific top-up scheme was found. It is possible a general A&N
   post-matric scheme exists and simply wasn't surfaced by the documents this
   session could reach — this should be treated as "not found," not as
   confirmation that no such scheme exists.
3. A second, very well-documented government institution was also found this
   session and could substitute for or supplement DBRAIT above if the pilot
   later wants a medical-track example for this UT: **ANIIMS (Andaman & Nicobar
   Islands Institute of Medical Sciences), Sri Vijaya Puram** — official 2026-27
   MBBS Prospectus PDF read in full via the same collegeadmission portal and
   local pdftotext method. Confirmed directly from that primary document:
   established 2015 under society ANIMERS; from 2026-27 a constituent college of
   Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Institute of Higher Learning (deemed university),
   NMC-recognised; teaching hospital Govind Ballabh Pant Hospital; **114 total
   MBBS seats** for 2026-27 (17 All-India Quota via MCC, 97 State Quota of which
   10 are EWS, with the remaining 87 split across five A&N-residency categories
   per a 1996 Ministry of Home Affairs distribution order); admission strictly
   NEET(UG)-2026 rank-based, minimum 50th percentile (40th for ST/OBC-NCL, 45th
   for PwBD-general/40th PwBD-ST/OBC); and a detailed one-time/refundable/
   per-semester fee table totalling **Rs 79,100 payable at admission** (tuition
   alone Rs 18,000/semester). This was not written up as the main entry only
   because the task asked for one institution and no ANIIMS-linked UT-run
   scholarship (as opposed to DBRAIT's prospectus, which named one) was found
   in the same document. A future session or reviewer could extend this file
   or create a companion one for ANIIMS.
4. Andaman & Nicobar Islands does have identifiable, distinct government
   higher-education infrastructure of its own (DBRAIT, ANIIMS, and several
   other institutions listed on the official `andamannicobar.gov.in` portal:
   Mahatma Gandhi Govt. College Mayabunder, Andaman Law College, Jawaharlal
   Nehru Rajkeeya Mahavidyalaya, Andaman College, Tagore Govt. College of
   Education, plus ITI Dollygunj and a DIET/TTI) — this is not a UT with no
   admissions infrastructure of its own. What is genuinely thin, per this
   session's research, is confirmed detail on its *own* scholarship
   infrastructure specifically, as distinct from centrally-run schemes routed
   through it.
