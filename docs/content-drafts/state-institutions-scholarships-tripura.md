## 2. State-administered post-matric scholarship — ST Post-Matric
   Scholarship, Tribal Welfare Department, Government of Tripura

**Honesty note up front:** this scheme is state-**administered** (the
Tripura Tribal Welfare Department issues the notification and runs
institute/district/state-level verification) but it is a Centrally
Sponsored Scheme (CSS) — centrally designed and funded under the Ministry
of Tribal Affairs' "Umbrella Scheme for Education of ST Children" — and
applications are submitted through the central National Scholarship
Portal (NSP), not a distinct Tripura-only portal. This mirrors the
pattern already flagged in comparable BCION Lite drafts for other states;
it is disclosed here rather than presented as a purely state-funded
scheme.

## Honest scope note

Tripura does have distinct admissions/scholarship infrastructure of its
own: a wholly government-established medical college (AGMC), created by a
direct Government-of-India Letter of Permission to the Government of
Tripura in 2005, with its own official website, its own (though dated)
state Health & Family Welfare Department fee notification, and its own
recognition-letter trail; and two separate state welfare departments
(Tribal Welfare, and Scheduled Castes Welfare) that each issue their own
dated scholarship notifications and run their own institute/district/
state-level verification chains for post-matric scholarships, rather than
students applying to a purely central scheme with no Tripura-specific
role. This is not a case of a small state with no infrastructure of its
own. The main gaps in this draft are precision and currency, not
absence: neither scholarship notification states an amount or (for the
SC scheme) an income cap, the college's newest located fee notification
is from 2013, and no Tripura government **engineering** institution was
researched this session (the task's "OR" was resolved in favour of the
medical institution, which was more directly documented from official
sources within this session's search budget).
