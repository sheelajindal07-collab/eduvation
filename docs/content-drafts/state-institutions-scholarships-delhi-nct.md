**Honest summary for this section:** across the official Delhi-government
pages this session could reach (Social Welfare Dept., Directorate of
Education, e-District portal, NSP homepage), no distinct, GNCTD-branded
post-matric scholarship scheme for Class 11-12/UG students — with its own
stated income cap and award amount — was found. The e-District portal's
own routing of SC/ST welfare applicants to the central NSP is a genuine
signal that Delhi may rely primarily on centrally-administered NSP
post-matric schemes rather than running a separately-branded state scheme,
but this session could not open and read an actual NSP scheme-guideline
document restricted to Delhi to confirm that directly, and one department
site (wcddel.in) was unreachable. This should be treated as "not found /
inconclusive," not as a confirmed finding that no such scheme exists.
