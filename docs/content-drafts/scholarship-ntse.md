## 0. IMPORTANT — current status could not be confirmed as "running"; this
   needs manual verification before NTSE is shown to students as an
   active opportunity

The official NCERT page and its linked notices/results show no evidence of
an NTSE exam cycle for new candidates having actually been conducted after
the 2021 cycle. The most recent exam-conduct evidence found is the Stage-II
2021 final result and a postponement notice for the 2021-22 cycle. No
2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 result or fresh exam notification could be
located on the official NCERT NTSE page this session. A contact-details
notice dated 04.06.2025 exists on NCERT's Educational Survey Division
letterhead for "NTSS" scholarship/exam enquiries (file no.
1-17(4)/2024-25/ESD/NTS-II), which shows the scheme is still administered
on paper as of mid-2025, but it does not itself confirm a new exam is
being held. Coaching-industry secondary sources (PhysicsWallah, FIITJEE)
describe NTSE as ongoing and list a "next" exam date, but PhysicsWallah's
own page states its date is "based on previous year patterns" (i.e. a
guess, not sourced to an official notification) and neither site was
treated as authoritative here. **Do not present NTSE as a currently
running opportunity for students until a verifier confirms directly with
NCERT/the state Liaison Officer whether Stage-I/Stage-II exams for the
current academic year have actually been notified.**
