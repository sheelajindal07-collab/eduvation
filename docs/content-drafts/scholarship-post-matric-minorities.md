## 2. Eligibility — marks and family income cap

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum marks requirement (fresh applicant) | Not less than 50% marks or equivalent grade in the previous final examination | Same PDF, Section 4 "Eligibility" | 2026-09-21 | High — read verbatim |
| Family income cap | Annual income of parents/guardians from all sources must NOT exceed ₹2.00 lakh | Same PDF, Section 4 "Eligibility" | 2026-09-21 | High — read verbatim. | Renewal condition (marks) | 50% marks in the previous year's examination, in the same course and same institute/school, verified by all designated authorities and approved by the State/UT | Same PDF, Sections 7 and 11(iv) | 2026-09-21 | High |
