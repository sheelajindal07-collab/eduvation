## 2. Eligibility — Flying Branch, AFCAT entry (Short Service Commission)

## 6. AFCAT written exam pattern (from the official "Scheme of Syllabus" document)

This table is sourced from the PDF linked as "Syllabus and Schemes of AFCAT"

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Number of questions and duration | 100 questions; total time allotted is two hours | https://afcat.edcil.co.in/assets/images/guidelines/download/afcatsektsyllabus.pdf ("AFCAT: Scheme of Syllabus", official PDF linked from https://afcat.edcil.co.in/guidelines.html) | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the primary PDF |
| Marking scheme | +3 marks for every correct answer; −1 mark for every incorrect answer; 0 marks for an un-attempted question; scores are normalised across candidates whenever the exam runs in more than one shift | Same PDF | 2026-09-21 | High |
| Sections and topics covered | (1) **English** — comprehension, error detection, sentence completion, synonym/antonym, cloze test, idioms and phrases, analogy, sentence rearranging, substitution, sentence transformation, homonyms. (3) **Numerical Ability** — decimal fractions, time & work, average/percentage, profit & loss, ratio & proportion, simple/compound interest, time & distance, races, area & perimeter, probability, number system & series, mixtures & allegation, squares/cubes & roots, HCF & LCM, elementary mensuration, heights & distances, statistics (mean/median/mode), exponents & powers.
