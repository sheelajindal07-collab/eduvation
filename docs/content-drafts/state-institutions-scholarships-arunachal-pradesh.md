## 1. National Institute of Technology Arunachal Pradesh (NIT AP) — government engineering institution

NIT AP is a central government Institute of National Importance (est. 2010,
under the Ministry of Education, NIT Act) located at Jote, Papum Pare
district. It is a central, not state-run, institution — flagged here
because it is the most prominent government engineering institution
physically located in Arunachal Pradesh and is a realistic pathway option
for a student from the state. An alternative candidate, Tomo Riba Institute
of Health & Medical Sciences (TRIHMS, Naharlagun) — the state's own
government medical college — was also checked; see the note at the end of
this section for why it was not used as the primary entry.

**Note on the alternative candidate (TRIHMS):** Tomo Riba Institute of Health
& Medical Sciences, Naharlagun, is Arunachal Pradesh's own state government
medical college (first and only one in the state), which would arguably be
a better fit than NIT AP for a strictly "state institution" theme. It was
investigated this session but dropped as the primary pick because: (a) the
institute's own official site (trihms.co.in) returned almost no readable
content beyond a bare "Welcome Trihms" homepage banner this session, so no
fact about it below could be sourced to a primary document; (b) secondary
sources actively conflict on MBBS seat count (100 seats per Wikipedia and a
June 2024 medicaldialogues.in report of an NMC approval doubling seats from
50 to 100, versus 50 seats — 15% AIQ / 85% state quota — per another
current secondary aggregator) and on annual fee (₹43,000/year per one
aggregator vs. ≈₹0.8 lakh/year per another). None of these figures could be
confirmed against a primary NMC, DME-Arunachal Pradesh, or TRIHMS document
this session, so they are omitted from the main table above rather than
presented as if sourced. A verifier revisiting this topic should try
trihms.co.in's inner pages directly, or the Directorate of Medical
Education, Training & Research, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, before
treating TRIHMS figures as usable.

## 3. Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) for ST students under Umbrella Scheme — companion scheme, centrally funded (not state-run)

Included because the same official DHTE notice lists it side-by-side with
the state scheme above as one of only two schemes AP students can apply
through on NSP, and because it is the one scheme in this file with fully
primary-confirmed income cap and amount figures. This scheme is centrally
sponsored by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs (100% central assistance), not
state-funded, even though the State Government of Arunachal Pradesh
administers/disburses it — do not describe it as "state-run" in any
published copy.
