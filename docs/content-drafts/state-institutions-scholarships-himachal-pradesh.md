### Honesty note on "state-run"

Himachal Pradesh does have its own distinct scholarship administration
(the Higher Education Department runs its own branded ePASS portal,
separate from a bare pass-through of the National Scholarship Portal), so
this is a genuine state-administered channel, not an invented one. However,
per the primary source read this session, the specific scheme reported in
section 2 (income-capped Post-Matric Scholarship for SC students) is a
**centrally sponsored** scheme that Himachal Pradesh *administers* — the
same pattern used for the Gujarat BCK-6.1 example in
`docs/content-drafts/gujarat-scholarships.md`. This session did not find a
wholly state-funded, income-capped post-matric scholarship distinct from
the centrally sponsored ones; the wholly state-funded schemes found
(section 2's last row) are merit-based prizes for toppers, not post-matric
income-based support. A verifier should decide which of the two scheme
types the product actually needs before publication.
