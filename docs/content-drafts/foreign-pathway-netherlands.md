## 3. Financial means ("study norm") requirement

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What the requirement is | Student must show "sufficient means of support" equal to 12 × the monthly "study norm" for one year of study; this amount does **not** include tuition/college fees, and is defined as equal to the maximum Dutch students can receive in study-benefit support while living in a dorm. | https://ind.nl/en/income-requirements-study | 2026-09-21 | High (automated-fetch caveat as above) |
| Study norm amount, 2026 (HBO/university level) | € 1,130.77 per month (≈ € 13,569 for a full year), stated as valid 1 January–31 December 2026. A different, lower figure (€ 928.58/month) applies to secondary/MBO-level education, which does not apply to a bachelor's applicant. | https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the official IND page; this figure also matched an independent secondary-source blog's reported number, which corroborates it, though the verifier should still re-open the live page since these amounts change every 1 January. |
| How this figure is used | This is a **regulatory floor for visa purposes only**, not a researched cost-of-living budget. Actual living costs (rent, food, transport, health insurance) in Dutch cities may be higher or lower than this floor; this session did not check an independent Dutch cost-of-living dataset (e.g. Nibud), so no such figure is asserted here. | — | 2026-09-21 | Not verified — "not found" (flagged, not asserted) |
| Accepted proof of funds | Personal bank statements, a deposit into the institution's account, a scholarship declaration, or a financial-support statement from a company/private sponsor or Netherlands-based financier; documents must be dated within 3 months and in Dutch, English, German or French. | https://ind.nl/en/income-requirements-study | 2026-09-21 | Medium |
