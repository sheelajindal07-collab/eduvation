## 2. Engineering admission — counselling body and process

| Fact | Value | Source | Access date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Counselling body | Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (JCECEB), based in Namkum, Ranchi — established under the Bihar Re-Constituted Act, 2000 (Jharkhand was carved out of Bihar in 2000, and JCECEB's legal basis traces to that reorganisation) | https://jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in/ | 2026-09-21 | High — read directly from the official board homepage |
| How general B.Tech/engineering seats are allocated | For general B.Tech admission to Jharkhand's state government-recognised engineering colleges, there is **no separate state engineering entrance exam**. Seats are allocated on **JEE Main rank**: JCECEB runs a state-level online counselling process (multiple rounds — registration, choice filling, seat allotment) using a "state merit list" built from JEE Main scores/rank, restricted to state-quota-eligible candidates | https://jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in/ (primary — states "Engineering (JEE Main-based admissions)" among the exams/processes it runs) | 2026-09-21 | High for "counselling body = JCECEB, based on JEE Main" (confirmed on the primary site); Medium for the round-by-round mechanics (corroborated by several secondary aggregators — careers360, ummid.com, shiksha — describing a 2026 cycle with registration in June–July and 3 counselling rounds, but not read from an official JCECEB information bulletin PDF this session) |
| What the "JCECE" exam itself actually is | JCECE (the Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination proper, as an entrance *test*) is reported by secondary sources to now cover only Agriculture and allied PCB/PCM-group courses — B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture, B.V.Sc & AH, B.Sc (Hons) Forestry/Horticulture, B.Tech Dairy Technology, B.Tech Agricultural Engineering, B.F.Sc — not general engineering | Secondary sources only (careers360/aglasem-derived search synthesis); not independently confirmed against an official JCECEB syllabus/bulletin PDF this session | 2026-09-21 | Medium — consistent across multiple secondary sources and consistent with the primary site's framing that engineering itself is "JEE Main-based," but needs a verifier to open the official JCECE 2026 information bulletin to confirm the exact course list |
| Related, narrower entrance routes mentioned by secondary sources | JCECE-LE (lateral entry to 2nd year B.Tech); a separate polytechnic/diploma entrance sometimes referred to as PECE | Secondary sources only | 2026-09-21 | Low — not found in primary-source form this session; "needs manual verification" |
| Central institutes are a separate track | NIT Jamshedpur (a centrally-funded institute, not a state college) admits B.Tech students via **JoSAA/CSAB** counselling using JEE Main rank (JEE Advanced is for IITs, not NITs), which is entirely separate from JCECEB's state counselling. Secondary sources describe a JoSAA seat-matrix split for NIT Jamshedpur of roughly 50% Home State (Jharkhand)/50% Other State for the home-state quota mechanism that JoSAA applies at all NITs | Secondary sources (careers360/bodmaseducation-derived search synthesis); not confirmed against josaa.nic.in directly this session | 2026-09-21 | Medium — the JoSAA/NIT structure (Main-based, Home-State/Other-State split) is a well-established national pattern, but the exact percentage for NIT Jamshedpur specifically was not verified against josaa.nic.in this session |
| Eligibility basics (general/state-quota candidates) | Indian citizen; local/permanent resident of Jharkhand (for state-quota seats); passed Class 12 (Intermediate) or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics compulsory | Secondary sources only | 2026-09-21 | Medium — consistent across multiple secondary sources, not read from a primary JCECEB eligibility bulletin this session |
