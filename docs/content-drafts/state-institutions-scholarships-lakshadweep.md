## Note on Lakshadweep's admissions infrastructure

Lakshadweep is India's smallest Union Territory by population (~64,000,
2011 census), spread across a small number of inhabited islands. This
session's official-site research found **no evidence of a Lakshadweep-based
government engineering college or a Lakshadweep-based MBBS/medical college**.
Instead, the UT's own administration publishes an annual "Checklist of MBBS
Seat for Kerala Origin Settled in Lakshadweep" notice
(https://lakshadweep.gov.in/notice/checklist-of-mbbs-seat-for-kerala-origin-setteled-in-lakshadweep/,
accessed 2026-09-21, window shown as 21 Sep–10 Oct 2026), which strongly
suggests Lakshadweep-origin students access MBBS seats through a
Kerala-administered quota/institution rather than a college on the islands
themselves. This looks like a genuine structural fact (small UT relying on a
neighbouring state for a professional-course seat pathway), not a research
gap — but the underlying PDF was not opened this session, so the mechanism
is not confirmed in detail below.

The one confirmed, distinctly Lakshadweep-based institution found is a
**government nursing college** (medical field), covered in section 1.
