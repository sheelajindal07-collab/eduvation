### Honest conclusion for Part 1

Ladakh does **not** appear to have a distinct, well-known, government-run
**degree-level** engineering college or **any** medical college of its
own, based on everything this session could read from official UT
sources. The most defensible "government engineering institution" answer
is **Government Polytechnic College, Leh** (diploma-level, UT-government
run, confirmed to exist), offered with that caveat rather than a diploma
college being presented as equivalent to a B.Tech college. This is a
legitimate small-UT finding, not an invented substitute — consistent with
the task's own allowance for states/UTs with little distinct admissions
infrastructure of their own.

## Honest scope note

Ladakh is a genuine case of a small UT with **limited, but not zero,**
distinct infrastructure of its own. It has no confirmed government
engineering-degree or medical college — the "Central Pool MBBS & BDS
Seats" scheme exists precisely because Ladakhi students need reserved
seats in colleges outside the UT. The closest defensible "engineering
institution" answer is the diploma-level Government Polytechnic College,
Leh, offered with that caveat rather than overstated as a B.Tech college.
On the scholarship side, Ladakh does run its own named UT scheme (REWA),
administered by the UT's Social Welfare Department — this is not a "no
infrastructure" finding — but this session could not read REWA's actual
income cap, amount or full eligibility from the page fetched, and all
three of its application tracks were shown closed at the time of the
fetch. Both gaps are recorded as "not found" rather than estimated or
substituted with a national scheme presented as Ladakh-specific.
