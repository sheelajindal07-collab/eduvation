## Honest scope note

Jammu and Kashmir does have its own distinct, named state institutions in
both categories the task asked about: SKIMS is a real, government-run
tertiary medical institution with a confirmed active MBBS programme
(confirmed via its own dated exam/result notices, not just a course
list), and the Directorate of Social Welfare (Kashmir) is a real J&K
state department running a named, state-specific Post Matric Scholarship
covering EBC/OBC/PCP/DNT (and, per a linked application page, SC)
categories, distinct from the central National Scholarship Portal that
the same department also links to. This is not a "no infrastructure"
case.

The gaps in this draft are about **access depth**, not absence: this
session's general web-search budget was already exhausted before this
topic began, direct fetches of public search engines returned unusable
results, and several guessed official domains for other candidate
institutions (GMC Srinagar, GMC Jammu, the parent J&K Social Welfare
Department's `.nic.in` site) did not resolve. As a result: SKIMS's MBBS
admission route, fee structure and seat intake were not found; the
Post Matric Scholarship's income cap, amount, exact class-range wording
and current-cycle (2026-27) status were not found; and it is unresolved
whether SC-category J&K students should use this state scheme or the
central NSP for post-matric scholarships. All of these are marked
"not found" above rather than estimated.
