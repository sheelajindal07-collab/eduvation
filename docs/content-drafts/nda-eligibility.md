## Cycle warning — read first

UPSC runs this exam twice a year (an "I" cycle and a "II" cycle), and the
eligible birth-date window shifts with each cycle because the NDA course
itself starts twice a year. The figures below were read directly from the
two most recent official notifications on `upsc.gov.in` at access time —
**NDA & NA (I), 2026** (Examination Notice No. 3/2026-NDA-I, notified
10.12.2025, exam held 12.04.2026, admits to the 157th NDA Course /
119th Indian Naval Academy Course commencing 01.01.2027) and **NDA & NA
(II), 2026** (Examination Notice No. 10/2026-NDA-II, notified 20.05.2026,
exam scheduled 13.09.2026, admits to the 158th NDA Course / 120th Indian
Naval Academy Course commencing 01.07.2027). Both cycles were already
closed for fresh applications by the access date. **A reviewer must check
`upsc.gov.in` for the next open notification (likely "NDA & NA (I),
2027") before publishing any specific date to a student**, and should
verify the *rule pattern* (unmarried, PCM-for-two-wings, etc.) still
holds, since UPSC republishes largely the same rule text each cycle but
the exact dates always change.
